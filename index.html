FAQs

Straight Answers, No Guesswork

Answers to the questions Tecumseh homeowners ask us most.

Are you licensed plumbers in Tecumseh?

Yes. Tecumseh Plumbing Pros works under a plumbing licence across Essex County, carries full insurance, and files permits for every job the Ontario Building Code requires them for.

Do you take emergency calls in Tecumseh?

Yes. Flooding, burst pipes, sewer backup, and total loss of water are priority dispatches, and our crews cover the Tecumseh shoreline and inland streets every day.

How fast can you replace a leaking water heater?

Most Tecumseh replacements happen same day or next morning, because the common tank sizes travel on our trucks. Drain, swap, permit, and haul-away in one visit.

We converted our cottage to year-round. Does the plumbing need a rethink?

Probably. Seasonal-grade pipe runs, drain layouts, and shutoff placement rarely survive real winters. We assess the whole system and repipe what freeze and use have outgrown.

Do you work on wells around Tecumseh?

Yes. Properties past the built edge on private wells get complete treatment: testing, softeners where hardness calls for it, plus iron, sulphur, and UV systems where the well does.

Is Tecumseh water hard on fixtures?

The municipal supply is moderately hard, enough to wear cartridges and cloud glassware over a few years. A properly sized softener settles that, and we test before selling capacity.

What does your quote include?

Everything the job needs to be finished and inspected, explained before work begins. The number we quote is the number you pay, whether it is a repair or a full repipe.

Do you go beyond Tecumseh itself?

Yes. Windsor, Lakeshore, Belle River, Stoney Point, LaSalle, and Essex are all regular territory for our crews.

Do you serve areas outside of Tecumseh, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Tecumseh and nearby communities including Windsor, Lakeshore, LaSalle, Stoney Point, and the surrounding area.
